Miner Automation

Sentinel runs these schedules and restarts on its next check after you save. Times are in Sentinel's display timezone: .

    Schedules

    A sleep window stops mining and wakes the miner when it ends. A power window switches the miner to a power mode or power target; outside every window the miner keeps its last mode, so pair windows to cover the day. When windows overlap, the first one in the list wins.

    Miners

    Stored credentials are write-only and used only by Sentinel. Without them Sentinel tries the firmware's default login. Avalon power modes need the model set; Avalons cannot sleep.

    Auto-restart

    Sentinel restarts a miner that stays offline, or below its expected hashrate, for this long. Miners asleep on a schedule are never restarted.
